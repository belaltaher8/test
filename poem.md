# A Nice Poem

## The World in Quiet Moments

Between the heartbeats of the day,
the world exhales a gentle sigh—
a leaf that spirals on its way,
a cloud that wanders through the sky.

The morning light on silver dew,
the hush before the songbird sings,
these ordinary things made new
remind us beauty quietly clings

to every corner, every hour,
to stone and stream and open field,
to the unassuming wildflower
that will not wilt and will not yield.

So pause a moment, breathe it in—
the world is wider than it seems.
Beneath the noise and all the din
there lives a world of golden dreams.
