# A Poem

The stars dissolve at break of day,
And morning trades the dark for gold;
The rivers run their ancient way,
And stories new repeat the old.

A leaf falls quiet to the ground,
The wind moves on without a name;
In silence there is always sound,
In every change, a thing the same.

We write our names in water, still
We reach for what we cannot keep;
And yet there's joy in trying, till
The light runs out and worlds must sleep.
